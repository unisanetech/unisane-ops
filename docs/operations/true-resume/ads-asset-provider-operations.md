# True Resume Advertising Asset Provider Operations

Unisane Ops owns raw advertising media custody, normalization, provider validation,
upload planning, guarded upload or creative execution, and mutable provider receipts.
The product-owned registry supplies reviewed asset facts and creative intent; it does
not authorize a provider operation.

Private operational state belongs under `.unisane/marketing/assets/**` in the selected
operator workspace. It includes source media, normalized variants, previews, provider
references, and receipts. None is committed as product Memory.

Every live operation requires a validated product registry, an approved lifecycle,
source and license evidence, a ready dry-run receipt, a human approval reference, exact
account and production confirmations, and the provider-specific live executor. Provider
asset identifiers are written only to private provider-reference state. Campaign or ad
creation may consume them only after that guarded upload step succeeds.

Placement-specific image dimensions, video duration, Page actor, and Instagram actor
requirements are provider validation inputs. Product plans must contain reviewed copy
and real approved product asset ids; placeholder creative values never admit execution.
Upload plans and their provider receipts remain private operational state and are not
committed to this public operations family.
